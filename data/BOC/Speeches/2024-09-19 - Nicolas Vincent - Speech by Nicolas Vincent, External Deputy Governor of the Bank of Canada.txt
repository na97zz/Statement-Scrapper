OTTAWA – On Thursday, September 19, 2024, Nicolas Vincent, External Deputy Governor of the Bank of Canada, will speak before the Sherbrooke Chamber of Commerce.
Topic
Monetary policy decision-making: behind the scenes
Time
08:15 (Eastern Time)
Place
Club de golf Sherbrooke
1000 Rue Musset
Sherbrooke, QC
Lock-Up
At 06:30 (ET), journalists are invited to review copies of the speech, under embargo, at the Bank’s head office in Ottawa. Please use the Bank of Canada Museum entrance, located at 30 Bank Street (corner of Bank and Wellington), and bring photo ID.
For security reasons, journalists wishing to attend must confirm their presence by contacting
Media Relations
before noon (ET) on Wednesday, September 18, 2024. Those who have not registered will not be admitted to the lock-up.
The embargo will be lifted at 08:00 (ET).
Distribution
The External Deputy Governor’s text will be available on the Bank’s
website
at 08:00 (ET).
Media Availability
There will be no media availability for this event.
Audience Q&A
There will be an audience Q&A period.

webcasts
