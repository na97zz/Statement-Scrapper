OTTAWA – On Tuesday, November 26, 2024, Rhys Mendes, Deputy Governor of the Bank of Canada, will speak before the Greater Charlottetown Area Chamber of Commerce.
Topic
Inflation at 2%: the role of monetary policy going forward
Time
09:20 (Atlantic Time), 08:20 (Eastern Time)
Place
Atlantic Technology Centre Theatre
176 Great George Street
Charlottetown, PE
Lock-Up
At 06:35 (ET), journalists are invited to review copies of the speech, under embargo, at the Bank’s head office in Ottawa. Please use the Bank of Canada Museum entrance, located at 30 Bank Street (corner of Bank and Wellington), and bring photo ID.
For security reasons, journalists wishing to attend must confirm their presence by contacting
Media Relations
before noon (ET) on Monday, November 25, 2024. Those who have not registered will not be admitted to the lock-up.
The embargo will be lifted at 08:05 (ET).
Distribution
The Deputy Governor’s text will be available on the Bank’s
website
at 09:05 (AT), 08:05 (ET).
Media Availability
There will be no media availability for this event.
Audience Q&A
There will be an audience Q&A period.
Webcast
Audio and video
webcasts
of the speech and media questions will be available.
Note
For more information, please contact
Media Relations
.
