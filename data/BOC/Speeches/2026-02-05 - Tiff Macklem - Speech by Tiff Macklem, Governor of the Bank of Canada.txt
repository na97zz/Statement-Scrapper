OTTAWA – On Thursday, February 5, 2026, Tiff Macklem, Governor of the Bank of Canada, will speak before the Empire Club of Canada.
Topic
Structural change – Canada at a crossroads
Time
12:40 (Eastern Time)
Place
Fairmont Royal York - Canadian Room
100 Front Street West
Toronto, ON
M5J 1E3
Lock-Up
At 10:55 (ET), journalists are invited to review copies of the speech, under embargo, at the Bank’s head office in Ottawa. Please use the Bank of Canada Museum entrance, located at 30 Bank Street (corner of Bank and Wellington), and bring photo ID.
For security reasons, journalists wishing to attend must confirm their presence by contacting
Media Relations
before noon (ET) on Wednesday, February 4, 2026. Those who have not registered will not be admitted to the lock-up.
The embargo will be lifted at 12:25 (ET).
Distribution
The Governor’s text will be available on the Bank’s
website
at 12:25 (ET).
Media Availability
At approximately 14:00 (ET), the Governor will hold a press conference in Ontario room.
Accredited journalists who wish to participate remotely must contact
Media Relations
for connection information before noon (ET) on Wednesday, February 4, 2026.
Audience Q&A
There will be a moderated Q&A period.

webcasts
of the speech and press conference will be available.
