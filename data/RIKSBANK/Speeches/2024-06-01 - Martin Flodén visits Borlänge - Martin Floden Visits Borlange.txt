Martin Flodén visits Borlänge
Deputy Governor Martin Flodén visited Borlänge on Tuesday and spoke about the Riksbank's role in the economy and monetary policy at a guest lecture at Dalarna University.

“In recent months, it has become increasingly clear that inflation is falling back towards the target. Last week's inflation outcome reinforces this picture. There is still a possible risk of setbacks, but the risk of inflation becoming entrenched at high levels has diminished,” said Mr Flodén.
Mr Flodén also met with local businesses and entrepreneurs and visited SSAB through an event organised by the Chamber of Commerce for Central Sweden.
